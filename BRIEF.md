# Roca Fuels — Website Build Brief (consolidated)

**Client:** Roca Fuels — energy/fuel retail division of ROCA Holdings (Riyadh HQ, est. 2005, 7 companies across KSA/UAE/Qatar/India). Premium dealer of **MRPL** (subsidiary of **ONGC**). First station: Calicut, Kerala; ambition: chain across South India.
**Deadline:** live on real domain 2026-07-25. **Stack:** Next.js (App Router, `output: 'export'` static), TypeScript, Tailwind, GSAP (+ScrollTrigger). Host: Cloudflare Pages. **Mobile-first** (on-the-go usage), fast loads, Calicut/Kerala local SEO basics (meta, JSON-LD GasStation schema).

- **Drop-gradient accent palette** (use sparingly — logo, icon accents, dividers, highlights, never large blocks): `#1C59C5 #009CC4 #006B84 #15B98C #8BBA44 #76A01C #F5CA00 #FF9F13 #CD6C00 #FF6632 #FD2C3A`.

- Photography: `..\Roca Fuels Assets\Images\` — `Pump Photos\` (6 forecourt/pump shots) + `Inauguration Photos\` (18 launch-event shots). Compress to webp.
- Vibe: trust, precision, energy. Navy atmosphere, gradient used like jewelry. GSAP: hero reveal, subtle gradient shimmer on the drop motif, scroll-reveals, stat/counters.
